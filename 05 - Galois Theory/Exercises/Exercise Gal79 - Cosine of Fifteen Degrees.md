# Exercise Gal79: Cosine of Fifteen Degrees

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.1
> Express $\cos15^\circ$ in terms of real square roots.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Write $15^\circ=45^\circ-30^\circ$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The subtraction formula gives
>
> $$
> \cos15^\circ
> =\cos45^\circ\cos30^\circ+\sin45^\circ\sin30^\circ.
> $$
>
> Substituting the familiar values yields
>
> $$
> \cos15^\circ
> =\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\frac{\sqrt3}{2}
> +\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\frac12
> =\boxed{\frac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}}.
> $$
