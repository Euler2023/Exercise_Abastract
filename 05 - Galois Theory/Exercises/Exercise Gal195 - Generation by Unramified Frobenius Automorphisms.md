# Exercise Gal195: Generation by Unramified Frobenius Automorphisms

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Neukirch VI.3.2
> If $L|K$ is a finite Galois extension, then the Galois group $G(L|K)$ is generated by the Frobenius automorphisms $\varphi_{\mathfrak P}$ of all prime ideals $\mathfrak P$ of $L$ which are unramified over $K$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint
> The subgroup generated by all these elements is normal. Its fixed field has almost every prime split completely.

> [!hint]- Stronger hint
> The fixed field of the normal subgroup generated by all Frobenius elements has almost every prime completely split, so apply VI.(3.8).

## Solution

> [!success]- Independent derivation
> Let $N$ be the subgroup generated by the listed Frobenius elements. For $\sigma\in G$, we have $\sigma\varphi_\mathfrak P\sigma^{-1}=\varphi_{\sigma\mathfrak P}$, so $N\triangleleft G$. Write $F=L^N$.
> For every prime $\mathfrak p$ unramified in $L/K$, its Frobenius in $F/K$ is the image of $\varphi_\mathfrak P$ modulo $N$ and is therefore the identity. All these primes split completely in $F/K$.
> **Cited result proved in the book: VI.(3.8), printed p.379/PDF398.** A finite extension in which almost all primes split completely is trivial. This gives $F=K$ and hence $N=G$.
