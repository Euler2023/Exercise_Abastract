# Exercise Gal44: A Generator for the Quartic Subfield of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_{17})$

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 10.2
> Let $\zeta=\zeta_{17}$. Find generators for the intermediate field $L_2$ of Example 16.10.3.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> $L_2$ is fixed by $\langle\sigma^4\rangle$, where $\sigma(\zeta)=\zeta^3$; take orbit sums.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The subgroup $\langle\sigma^4\rangle$ has order $4$. Its four orbits on nonzero exponents modulo $17$ are the cosets of $\langle3^4\rangle=\{1,4,13,16\}$. Hence $L_2$ is generated by any nontrivial period, for example
> $$
> \eta=\zeta+\zeta^4+\zeta^{13}+\zeta^{16}.
> $$
> Its $G$-orbit consists of the four sums attached to the four cosets, so $[\mathbb Q(\eta):\mathbb Q]=4=[L_2:\mathbb Q]$. Thus $L_2=\mathbb Q(\eta)$. Equivalently one may use the two-stage generator $\sqrt{17}$ together with $\eta$.
