# Exercise Gal59: A Three-Cycle in a Quintic Galois Group

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 12.3
> Let $G$ be the Galois group of an irreducible quintic. Show that if $G$ contains an element of order $3$, then $G=S_5$ or $A_5$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Use the list of transitive subgroups of $S_5$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Irreducibility makes $G$ transitive. The transitive subgroups are
> $$
> C_5,\ D_5,\ F_{20},\ A_5,\ S_5.
> $$
> The first three have orders $5,10,20$, not divisible by $3$, so contain no element of order $3$. Therefore only $A_5$ and $S_5$ remain.

The order-three element in $S_5$ is necessarily a 3-cycle.
