# Exercise Gal23: Realizing an $S_3$-Extension by a Cubic

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 7.6
> If $K/F$ is Galois with group $S_3$, is $K$ the splitting field of an irreducible cubic over $F$?

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Take the fixed field of a subgroup generated by a transposition.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Yes. Let $H\le S_3$ be an order-two subgroup. Its fixed field $L=K^H$ has degree $[S_3:H]=3$ over $F$. Choose $\alpha$ with $L=F(\alpha)$; its minimal polynomial is irreducible cubic. The splitting field inside $K$ is fixed by the core $\bigcap_{g\in G}gHg^{-1}$. The three transposition subgroups have trivial intersection, so the core is trivial. Hence the splitting field is all of $K$.
