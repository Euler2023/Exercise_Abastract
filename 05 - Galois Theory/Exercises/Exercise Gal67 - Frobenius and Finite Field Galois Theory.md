# Exercise Gal67: Frobenius and Finite Field Galois Theory

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise M.5
> For $K=\mathbb F_q$, $q=p^r$: (a) prove Frobenius $x\mapsto x^p$ is an automorphism over $\mathbb F_p$; (b) prove the Galois group is cyclic of order $r$, generated by Frobenius; (c) prove the Main Theorem for $K/\mathbb F_p$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The fixed field of $\varphi^d$ has $p^{\gcd(r,d)}$ elements.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> In characteristic $p$, $(x+y)^p=x^p+y^p$, so Frobenius is a homomorphism; it is injective and hence bijective on the finite field. It fixes $\mathbb F_p$.
>
> Every $\mathbb F_p$-automorphism sends an element to a conjugate under Frobenius. The powers $1,\varphi,\ldots,\varphi^{r-1}$ are distinct, while $\varphi^r=1$, so
> $$
> \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb F_{p^r}/\mathbb F_p)=\langle\varphi\rangle\cong C_r.
> $$
> For each divisor $d\mid r$, the unique subgroup of order $r/d$ has fixed field $\mathbb F_{p^d}$. Conversely every intermediate finite field has $p^d$ elements for some $d\mid r$. This is exactly the inclusion-reversing Galois correspondence.
