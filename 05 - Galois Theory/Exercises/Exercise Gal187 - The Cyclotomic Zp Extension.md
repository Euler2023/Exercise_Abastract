# Exercise Gal187: The Cyclotomic Zp Extension

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Neukirch V.1.7
> Let $p$ be the residue characteristic of $K$, and let $L$ be the field generated by all roots of unity of $p$-power order. The fixed field of the torsion subgroup of $G(L/K)$ is a $\mathbb Z_p$-extension. It is called the cyclotomic $\mathbb Z_p$-extension.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The cyclotomic character embeds $G(L/K)$ as an open subgroup of $\mathbb Z_p^*$.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> Use $\mathbb Z_p^*=\mu_{p-1}\times(1+p\mathbb Z_p)$ for odd $p$, and $\{\pm1\}\times(1+4\mathbb Z_2)$ for $p=2$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> **Proof scope:** Independent derivation
>
> Let $C=\mathbb Q_p(\mu_{p^\infty})$. Cyclotomic Galois theory identifies $G(C/\mathbb Q_p)$ with $\mathbb Z_p^*$. Since $K/\mathbb Q_p$ is finite, $K\cap C$ is finite over $\mathbb Q_p$ and is contained in a finite cyclotomic layer. Thus
> $$
> G(L/K)\cong G(C/(K\cap C))
> $$
> is open in $\mathbb Z_p^*$.
>
> For odd $p$, the logarithm identifies $1+p\mathbb Z_p$ with $p\mathbb Z_p$; for $p=2$ it identifies $1+4\mathbb Z_2$ with $4\mathbb Z_2$. In either case write $\mathbb Z_p^*=T_0\times P$, with $T_0$ finite and $P\cong\mathbb Z_p$. The projection of the open subgroup $H=G(L/K)$ to $P$ is an open subgroup of $P$, hence isomorphic to $\mathbb Z_p$. Its kernel is $H\cap T_0$, exactly the torsion subgroup of $H$. Consequently $H/H_{\rm tors}\cong\mathbb Z_p$. The fixed field of $H_{\rm tors}$ has this Galois group over $K$, proving the assertion.
