# Exercise Gal43: Degree of $\zeta_7$ over $\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 10.1
> Determine the degree of $\zeta_7$ over $\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Compare the quadratic subfield of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)$ with $\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The fields have degrees $6$ and $2$ over $\mathbb Q$. The unique quadratic subfield of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)$ is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$, whereas $\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$. Hence their intersection is $\mathbb Q$, and
> $$
> [\mathbb Q(\zeta_3,\zeta_7):\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)]=[\mathbb Q(\zeta_7):\mathbb Q]=6.
> $$
