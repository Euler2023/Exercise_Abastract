# Exercise Gal47: Intermediate Fields of Prime Cyclotomic Fields

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 10.5
> For $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_p)$, determine explicitly all intermediate fields for $p=5,7,11,13$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The cyclic group of order $p-1$ has one subgroup for each divisor; use Gaussian periods.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> For every divisor $d\mid p-1$, let $H_d$ be the unique subgroup of $(\mathbb Z/p)^\times$ of order $d$, and set
> $$
> \eta_d=\sum_{a\in H_d}\zeta_p^a.
> $$
> Then $K^{H_d}=\mathbb Q(\eta_d)$, of degree $(p-1)/d$, and these are all fields.
>
> Thus: $p=5$ has degrees $1,2,4$, with quadratic field $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$; $p=7$ has degrees $1,2,3,6$, with quadratic $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$ and real cubic $\mathbb Q(\zeta_7+\zeta_7^{-1})$; $p=11$ has degrees $1,2,5,10$, with quadratic $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-11})$ and real quintic; $p=13$ has degrees $1,2,3,4,6,12$, generated by the corresponding period sums. The degree-six field is the maximal real subfield; the quadratic field is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{13})$.
