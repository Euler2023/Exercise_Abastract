# Exercise Gal50: Galois Groups of $x^8-1,x^{12}-1,x^9-1$

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 10.8
> Determine the Galois groups over $\mathbb Q$ of $x^8-1$, $x^{12}-1$, and $x^9-1$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Their splitting fields are cyclotomic fields.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The groups are the unit groups modulo the respective exponents:
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\zeta_8)/\mathbb Q)&\cong(\mathbb Z/8)^\times\cong C_2\times C_2,\\
> \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\zeta_{12})/\mathbb Q)&\cong(\mathbb Z/12)^\times\cong C_2\times C_2,\\
> \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\zeta_9)/\mathbb Q)&\cong(\mathbb Z/9)^\times\cong C_6.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
