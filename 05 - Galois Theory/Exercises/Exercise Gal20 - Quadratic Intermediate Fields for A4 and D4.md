# Exercise Gal20: Quadratic Intermediate Fields for $A_4$ and $D_4$

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 7.3
> How many $L$ with $[L:F]=2$ occur when the Galois group is (a) $A_4$, (b) $D_4$?

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Count index-two subgroups.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Index-two subgroups are kernels of nontrivial homomorphisms to $C_2$. The abelianization of $A_4$ is $C_3$, so there is no such homomorphism and the answer is $0$.
>
> For Artin's order-eight $D_4=\langle r,s\rangle$, the abelianization is $C_2\times C_2$. It has three nonzero homomorphisms to $C_2$, hence three index-two subgroups and three quadratic intermediate fields.
