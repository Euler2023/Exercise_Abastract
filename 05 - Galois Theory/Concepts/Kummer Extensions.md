# Kummer Extensions

## Definition

> [!info] Prime-degree Kummer extension
> Let $p$ be prime and let $F$ contain a primitive $p$-th root of unity $\zeta_p$. A degree-$p$ Galois extension $K/F$ is a Kummer extension if
> $$
> K=F(\beta),\qquad \beta^p\in F.
> $$

## Intuition

A generator $\sigma$ of the cyclic Galois group acts $F$-linearly on $K$. Because the relevant roots of unity are already in $F$, an eigenvector $\beta$ with $\sigma(\beta)=\zeta_p\beta$ has invariant $p$-th power.

## Key Properties

- Under the stated hypotheses, every degree-$p$ Galois extension is Kummer.
- $x^p-a$ is either irreducible or splits completely when $F$ contains $\zeta_p$.
- The roots-of-unity condition is essential.
- Composite-exponent Kummer theory classifies suitable abelian extensions using $F^\times/F^{\times m}$; that stronger theory is beyond Artin's prime-degree theorem.

## Example

> [!example] Cyclic cubic
> If $F$ contains $\omega$ and $a\notin F^{\times3}$, the splitting field of $x^3-a$ is $F(\sqrt[3]a)$, with group generated by $\sqrt[3]a\mapsto\omega\sqrt[3]a$.
