# Exercise AG62: Small Ray Class Characters and the Infinite Part

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Neukirch VII.6.4
> The characters of the “small ray class group” $J^{\mathfrak m}/\bar P^{\mathfrak m}$ mod $\mathfrak m$ are the Größencharaktere mod $\mathfrak m$ such that $\chi_\infty=1$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The subgroup $\bar P^{\mathfrak m}$ consists of principal ideals generated by $\alpha\equiv1\pmod{\mathfrak m}$, without a positivity condition.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> In one direction the defining identity gives triviality on $\bar P^{\mathfrak m}$. In the other direction the same congruence elements are dense in the archimedean group.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> **Proof scope:** Independent derivation
>
> Set $K^{\mathfrak m}=\{\alpha\in K^*: \alpha\equiv1\pmod{\mathfrak m}\}$, and let $\bar P^{\mathfrak m}=\{(\alpha):\alpha\in K^{\mathfrak m}\}$. A Größencharakter has
> $$
> \chi((\alpha))=\chi_f(\alpha)\chi_\infty(\alpha)
> $$
> for $\alpha$ prime to $\mathfrak m$, with reduction of fractions understood in $(\mathfrak o/\mathfrak m)^*$. If $\chi_\infty=1$ and $\alpha\in K^{\mathfrak m}$, then $\chi_f(\alpha)=1$ also. Therefore $\chi(\bar P^{\mathfrak m})=1$, and $\chi$ factors uniquely through the small ray class group.
>
> Conversely, let $\psi:J^{\mathfrak m}/\bar P^{\mathfrak m}\to S^1$ be a character, and pull it back to $\chi$ on $J^{\mathfrak m}$. Define $\chi_\infty=1$. For $u\in(\mathfrak o/\mathfrak m)^*$ choose an integral lift $a$ and set
> $$
> \chi_f(u)=\chi((a)).
> $$
> If $a,b$ are two lifts then $a/b\equiv1\pmod{\mathfrak m}$, so $(a)(b)^{-1}\in\bar P^{\mathfrak m}$; hence the definition is independent of the lift. Multiplicativity follows from $(ab)=(a)(b)$. It follows that $\chi((a))=\chi_f(a)\chi_\infty(a)$ for every integral $a$ prime to the modulus. This is a Größencharakter with trivial infinite part.
>
> For completeness, the associated infinite part is unique: for $\alpha\in K^{\mathfrak m}$ its value must be $\chi((\alpha))=1$. Weak approximation says that $K^{\mathfrak m}$ is dense in $R^*$, and continuity forces $\chi_\infty=1$ everywhere. This confirms that the criterion concerns the source's associated infinite part, rather than an arbitrarily chosen presentation.
>
> At $K=\mathbb Q$, $\mathfrak m=(1)$ the small ray group is trivial, and the only character with trivial finite and infinite parts is the trivial one. Imposing signs at real places instead would give the larger ray class group and a different criterion.
