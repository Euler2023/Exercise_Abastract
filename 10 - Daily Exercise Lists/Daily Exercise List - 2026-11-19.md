2. [ ] [[04 - Linear Algebra and Modules/Exercises/Exercise LA233 - Householder Reflections|Exercise LA233: Householder Reflections]] <!-- exercise-manager:systematic-unit 0922343711d27ac3248228a9aea4147102ca57dcdb7b8d95b8d0eeacbfbfeddf -->

   **Source:** Michael Artin, Algebra, 2nd ed., Ch. 8, Section 5, Ex. 5.3, printed p. 256, PDF p. 268

   > [!question] Exercise 5.3
   > Let $w\in\mathbb R^n$ be a vector of length $1$, and let $U=w^\perp$. The reflection $r_w$ about $U$ is defined by writing $v=cw+u$ with $u\in U$ and setting $r_w(v)=-cw+u$.
   >
   > **(a)** Prove that $P=I-2ww^{\mathsf T}$ is orthogonal.
   >
   > **(b)** Prove that multiplication by $P$ is reflection about $U$.
   >
   > **(c)** Let $u,v$ be vectors of equal length in $\mathbb R^n$. Determine a vector $w$ such that $Pu=v$.

3. [ ] [[04 - Linear Algebra and Modules/Exercises/Exercise LA234 - Symmetric Idempotents as Orthogonal Projections|Exercise LA234: Symmetric Idempotents as Orthogonal Projections]] <!-- exercise-manager:systematic-unit b057a86ee17a0bb2d1f28895e3c7f5a74c035f6ec7641d1b91bc799b18e06ef1 -->

   **Source:** Michael Artin, Algebra, 2nd ed., Ch. 8, Section 5, Ex. 5.4, printed p. 256, PDF p. 268

   > [!question] Exercise 5.4
   > Let $T$ be a linear operator on $V=\mathbb R^n$ whose matrix $A$ is real symmetric.
   >
   > **(a)** Prove that $V$ is the orthogonal sum $V=(\ker T)\oplus(\operatorname{im}T)$.
   >
   > **(b)** Prove that $T$ is an orthogonal projection onto $\operatorname{im}T$ if and only if, in addition to being symmetric, $A^2=A$.
