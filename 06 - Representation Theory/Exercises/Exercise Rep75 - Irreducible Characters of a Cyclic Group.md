# Exercise Rep75: Irreducible Characters of a Cyclic Group

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.6
> Let $G$ be a cyclic group of order $n$, generated by $x$, and let $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/n}$. **(a)** Prove that the irreducible representations are $\rho_0,\ldots,\rho_{n-1}$, where $\rho_k(x)=\zeta^k$. **(b)** Identify the character group of $G$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> A finite-order complex matrix is diagonalizable.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> In any complex representation, $\rho(x)^n=I$, and $t^n-1$ has distinct roots. Thus $\rho(x)$ is diagonalizable and the representation splits into one-dimensional eigenspaces. The irreducibles are therefore exactly $\rho_k(x)=\zeta^k$ for $0\le k<n$. Their products satisfy $\rho_j\rho_k=\rho_{j+k\bmod n}$, so $\widehat G$ is cyclic of order $n$, generated by $\rho_1$, and $\widehat G\cong C_n\cong G$. $\square$
