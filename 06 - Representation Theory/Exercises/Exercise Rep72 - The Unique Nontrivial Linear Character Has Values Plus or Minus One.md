# Exercise Rep72: The Unique Nontrivial Linear Character Has Values Plus or Minus One

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.3
> Suppose that a group $G$ has exactly two irreducible characters of dimension $1$, and let $\chi$ denote the nontrivial one-dimensional character. Prove that for all $g\in G$, $\chi(g)=\pm1$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The square $\chi^2$ is another one-dimensional character.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> One-dimensional characters form a group under pointwise multiplication. Since this group has exactly two elements, its nonidentity element $\chi$ has order $2$. Thus $\chi(g)^2=1$ for every $g$, and hence $\chi(g)=\pm1$. $\square$
