# Exercise Rep83: Outer Conjugation on the Irreducible Representations of A5

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 6.7
> The group $S_5$ operates by conjugation on its normal subgroup $A_5$. How does this action operate on the isomorphism classes of irreducible representations of $A_5$?

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Odd permutations interchange the two $A_5$-classes of $5$-cycles.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Conjugation by an element of $A_5$ is inner and fixes every character. An odd permutation interchanges the two conjugacy classes of $5$-cycles and fixes the other classes. In the $A_5$ table, the two degree-three characters differ exactly by interchanging their values on those two classes. Hence odd conjugation swaps $3$ and $3'$ and fixes the irreducibles of degrees $1,4,5$. Thus the $S_5/A_5\cong C_2$ action has one two-element orbit $\{3,3'\}$ and three fixed points. $\square$
