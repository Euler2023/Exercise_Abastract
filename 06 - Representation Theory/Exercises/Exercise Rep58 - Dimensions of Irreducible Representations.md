# Exercise Rep58: Dimensions of Irreducible Representations

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 4.1
> Find the dimensions of the irreducible representations of the octahedral group, the quaternion group, and the dihedral groups $D_4$, $D_5$, and $D_6$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Count conjugacy classes and use $\sum d_i^2=|G|$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The octahedral group is isomorphic to $S_4$, has order $24$ and five conjugacy classes. Its abelianization is $C_2$, giving two one-dimensional representations; the remaining squares total $22$, so the dimensions are $1,1,2,3,3$.
>
> Both $Q_8$ and $D_4$ have order $8$, five classes, and abelianization $C_2^2$. Thus each has dimensions $1,1,1,1,2$.
>
> The group $D_5$ has order $10$ and four classes. It has two one-dimensional characters, leaving $8=2^2+2^2$, so its dimensions are $1,1,2,2$.
>
> Finally $D_6$ has order $12$ and six classes. Its abelianization is $C_2^2$, and the remaining $8$ gives two two-dimensional representations. Its dimensions are $1,1,1,1,2,2$. $\square$
