# Exercise Rep97: Complexification of Quaternionic Left Multiplication

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 8.4
> Left multiplication defines a representation of $SU_2$ on the space $\mathbb R^4$ with coordinates $x_0,\ldots,x_3$, as in Section 9.3. Decompose the associated complex representation into irreducible representations.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Regard $\mathbb H$ as the underlying real space of the standard complex $SU_2$-module.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Identifying $\mathbb H\cong\mathbb C^2$, left multiplication is the underlying real representation of the standard complex module $V$. For any complex representation viewed as real,
>
> $$
> V_{\mathbb R}\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C\cong V\oplus\overline V.
> $$
>
> The standard $SU_2$ representation is self-conjugate: the invariant alternating form identifies $\overline V\cong V$. Hence the complexified four-dimensional representation decomposes as
>
> $$
> V\oplus V,
> $$
>
> two copies of the irreducible standard two-dimensional representation. $\square$
