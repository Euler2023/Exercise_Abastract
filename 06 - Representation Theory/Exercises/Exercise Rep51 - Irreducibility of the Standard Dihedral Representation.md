# Exercise Rep51: Irreducibility of the Standard Dihedral Representation

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 2.2
> Consider the standard two-dimensional representation of the dihedral group $D_n$. For which $n$ is this an irreducible complex representation?

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Diagonalize the rotation over $\mathbb C$.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> A reflection interchanges the two rotation eigenlines.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Let $r$ be rotation through $2\pi/n$ and let $s$ be a reflection. Over $\mathbb C$, $r$ has eigenvalues $\zeta$ and $\zeta^{-1}$, where $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/n}$. If $n\ge3$, these eigenvalues are distinct, so every $r$-invariant line is one of the two eigenlines. Since $srs=r^{-1}$, the reflection interchanges those lines. Neither is therefore invariant under all of $D_n$, and the representation is irreducible.
>
> For $n=1$ the action is generated by a reflection and splits into its two eigenspaces. For $n=2$, $r=-I$ and the reflection eigenspaces are again invariant. Thus the standard complex representation is irreducible exactly for $n\ge3$. $\square$
