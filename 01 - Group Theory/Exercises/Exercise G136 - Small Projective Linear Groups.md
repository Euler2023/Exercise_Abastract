# Exercise G136: Small Projective Linear Groups

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 8.2
> Describe isomorphisms $PSL_2(\mathbb F_2)\cong S_3$ and $PSL_2(\mathbb F_3)\cong A_4$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Let each group act by fractional linear transformations on $\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_q)$.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> For $q=3$, check the parity of the permutations induced by $z\mapsto z+1$ and $z\mapsto-1/z$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The natural action
>
> $$
> \begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}z=\frac{az+b}{cz+d}
> $$
>
> on $\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_q)$ has kernel the scalar matrices, so it is faithful on $PSL_2(\mathbb F_q)$.
>
> For $q=2$, the projective line has three points and $|PSL_2(\mathbb F_2)|=6$. The faithful action therefore identifies the group with all of $S_3$.
>
> For $q=3$, the projective line has four points and $|PSL_2(\mathbb F_3)|=12$. The group is generated by
>
> $$
> T:z\mapsto z+1,
> \qquad S:z\mapsto-1/z.
> $$
>
> On $(\infty,0,1,2)$, $T$ is the $3$-cycle $(0\ 1\ 2)$, while $S$ is the double transposition $(\infty\ 0)(1\ 2)$. Both are even, so the image lies in $A_4$. Its order is $12$, hence it equals $A_4$.
>
> $\square$
