# Exercise G324: Free Matrix Groups from Projective Ping-Pong

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 56 (Tits)
> Let $M_1,\ldots,M_r\in\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb C)$. Write $\lambda_i,\lambda_i'$ for the eigenvalues of $M_i$. Assume each $M_i$ has two distinct complex fixed points, $|\lambda_i|<|\lambda_i'|$, and all $2r$ fixed points are mutually distinct. Prove that some positive integer $k$ makes $M_1^k,\ldots,M_r^k$ free generators of a free subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb C)$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Choose disjoint discs around the attracting and repelling fixed points. Large positive powers contract toward one disc and large negative powers toward the other; then apply Exercise 54.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Let $w_i,w_i'$ be the fixed points belonging to $\lambda_i,\lambda_i'$, with $w_i'$ attracting for positive iteration because $|\lambda_i/\lambda_i'|<1$. Choose pairwise disjoint small discs $U_i$ about $w_i$ and $U_i'$ about $w_i'$, and put $S_i=U_i\cup U_i'$. Choose $s$ outside every $S_i$.
>
> Exercise 55, after conjugating each $M_i$ to multiplication by $\lambda_i/\lambda_i'$, gives an integer $K_i$ such that for every $m\ge K_i$,
>
> $$
> M_i^m\bigl(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb C)\setminus U_i\bigr)\subseteq U_i',
> \qquad
> M_i^{-m}\bigl(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb C)\setminus U_i'\bigr)\subseteq U_i.
> $$
>
> Because there are only finitely many matrices, take $k\ge\max_iK_i$ and set $G_i=\langle M_i^k\rangle$. Each $G_i$ is infinite cyclic: a nonzero power cannot be scalar because the eigenvalue ratio has modulus different from $1$. For every nonidentity $g\in G_i$, positive powers send every $S_j$ with $j\ne i$ and the base point $s$ into $U_i'$, while negative powers send them into $U_i$. Thus
>
> $$
> gS_j\subseteq S_i\quad(j\ne i),
> \qquad g(s)\in S_i.
> $$
>
> Exercise 54 applies and gives
>
> $$
> \langle M_1^k,\ldots,M_r^k\rangle
> \cong G_1*\cdots*G_r,
> $$
>
> the free group freely generated by $M_1^k,\ldots,M_r^k$.
