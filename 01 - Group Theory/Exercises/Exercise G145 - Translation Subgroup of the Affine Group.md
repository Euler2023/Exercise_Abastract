# Exercise G145: Translation Subgroup of the Affine Group

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Miscellaneous Exercise M.5
> The affine group $A_n$ is the group of transformations of $\mathbb R^n$ generated by $GL_n$ and the group $T_n$ of translations: $t_a(x)=x+a$. Prove that $T_n$ is a normal subgroup of $A_n$ and that $A_n/T_n$ is isomorphic to $GL_n$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Write every affine transformation uniquely as $x\mapsto Ax+b$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Every element of $A_n$ has a unique pair $(A,b)$ with
>
> $$
> (A,b)(x)=Ax+b,
> \qquad A\in GL_n(\mathbb R),\ b\in\mathbb R^n.
> $$
>
> The multiplication law is
>
> $$
> (A,b)(C,d)=(AC,Ad+b).
> $$
>
> Therefore
>
> $$
> (A,b)(I,a)(A,b)^{-1}=(I,Aa),
> $$
>
> so translations form a normal subgroup. The map
>
> $$
> \pi:A_n\longrightarrow GL_n(\mathbb R),
> \qquad \pi(A,b)=A,
> $$
>
> is a surjective homomorphism with kernel $T_n$. The First Isomorphism Theorem gives
>
> $$
> A_n/T_n\cong GL_n(\mathbb R).
> $$
>
> $\square$
