# Exercise G87: The Product of Two Infinite Cyclic Groups Is Not Cyclic

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 11.3
> Prove the product of two infinite cyclic groups is not infinite cyclic.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Identify the product with $\mathbb Z^2$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The product is isomorphic to $\mathbb Z^2$. If it were generated by $(a,b)$, then both $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ would be integer multiples of $(a,b)$. But all multiples of a single vector lie on one rational line, whereas $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ are linearly independent. Hence $\mathbb Z^2$ is not cyclic.
