# Exercise G305: Dihedral and Binary Dihedral Matrix Groups

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 34
> (a) Let $n$ be an even positive integer. Construct a group of order $2n$, generated by $\sigma,\tau$ with $\sigma^n=e=\tau^2$ and $\sigma\tau=\tau\sigma^{n-1}$. This is the dihedral group.
>
> (b) Let $n$ be odd, let $\zeta$ be a primitive $n$-th root of unity, and let $D_{4n}$ be generated by
>
> $$
> A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
> \qquad
> B=\begin{pmatrix}\zeta&0\\0&\zeta^{-1}\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Show that $D_{4n}$ has order $4n$ and give the commutation relations between $A$ and $B$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> For (a), use rotations and a reflection of a regular $n$-gon. For (b), compute $A^2$ and $ABA^{-1}$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> **(a)** Label the vertices of a regular $n$-gon cyclically. Let $\sigma$ rotate one step and let $\tau$ reflect across a symmetry axis. Then $\sigma^n=\tau^2=e$ and reflection reverses rotation:
>
> $$
> \tau\sigma\tau=\sigma^{-1},
> $$
>
> equivalently $\sigma\tau=\tau\sigma^{n-1}$. Every symmetry is uniquely $\sigma^j$ or $\tau\sigma^j$ for $0\le j<n$, so the group has order $2n$.
>
> **(b)** Direct multiplication gives
>
> $$
> A^2=-I,
> \qquad A^4=I,
> \qquad B^n=I,
> \qquad ABA^{-1}=B^{-1},
> $$
>
> or $AB=B^{-1}A$. These relations reduce every word to $B^jA^k$ with $0\le j<n$ and $0\le k<4$. Because $n$ is odd, $-I\notin\langle B\rangle$, so $\langle A\rangle\cap\langle B\rangle=1$ and all $4n$ normal forms are distinct. Thus $|D_{4n}|=4n$.
