# Exercise G210: Element Orders in S7

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.3
> Determine the orders of the elements of $S_7$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Take least common multiples of the parts of every partition of $7$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The order is the least common multiple of the disjoint-cycle lengths. The partitions of $7$ yield exactly
>
> $$
> 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,10,12.
> $$
>
> These occur for types $1^7$, $2,1^5$, $3,1^4$, $4,1^3$, $5,1^2$, $3,2,1^2$, $7$, $5,2$, and $4,3$. No other partition gives a new least common multiple. $\square$
