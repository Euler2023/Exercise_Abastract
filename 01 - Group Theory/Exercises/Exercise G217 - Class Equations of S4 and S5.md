# Exercise G217: Class Equations of S4 and S5

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.10
> Verify formulas (7.5.2) and (7.5.3) for the class equations of $S_4,S_5$, and determine a representative centralizer in each class.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Use cycle types and the centralizer-order formula.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> For $S_4$, types $1,2,2^2,3,4$ have centralizer orders $24,4,8,3,4$, so
>
> $$
> 24=1+6+3+8+6.
> $$
>
> The centralizers are $S_4$, $C_2\times S_2$, $D_4$, $C_3$, and $C_4$. For $S_5$, types $1,2,2^2,3,3\cdot2,4,5$ have centralizer orders $120,12,8,6,6,4,5$, giving
>
> $$
> 120=1+10+15+20+20+30+24.
> $$
>
> Their structures are $S_5,C_2\times S_3,D_4,C_3\times S_2,C_3\times C_2,C_4,C_5$. $\square$
