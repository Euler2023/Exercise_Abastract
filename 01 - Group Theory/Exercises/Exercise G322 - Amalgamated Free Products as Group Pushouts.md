# Exercise G322: Amalgamated Free Products as Group Pushouts

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 53
> Let $f:H\to G$ and $g:H\to G'$ be homomorphisms. Define the coproduct of these homomorphisms over $H$ and show that it exists.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Start with the free product $G*G'$ and impose the relations $f(h)=g(h)$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> A coproduct over $H$ is a group $P$ with homomorphisms $i:G\to P$ and $i':G'\to P$ such that $if=i'g$, universal with this property: whenever $u:G\to Q$ and $u':G'\to Q$ satisfy $uf=u'g$, there is a unique $v:P\to Q$ with $vi=u$ and $vi'=u'$.
>
> Let $G*G'$ be the free product with canonical maps $j,j'$, and let $N$ be the normal subgroup generated by
>
> $$
> j(f(h))j'(g(h))^{-1},
> \qquad h\in H.
> $$
>
> Put $P=(G*G')/N$ and let $i,i'$ be the induced maps. The defining generators of $N$ make $if=i'g$. Given a compatible pair $u,u'$, the universal property of $G*G'$ gives a unique homomorphism $\widetilde v:G*G'\to Q$. Compatibility makes $N\subseteq\ker\widetilde v$, so $\widetilde v$ descends uniquely to $P$. Therefore $P$ is the desired pushout, usually denoted $G*_HG'$ when $f,g$ are inclusions.
