# Exercise G271: One Relation with Coprime Coefficients

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 7.2
> Write the abelian group generated by $x$ and $y$, with the relation $3x+4y=0$, as a direct sum of cyclic groups.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The row $(3,4)$ has gcd one and can be completed to a unimodular matrix.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The presented group is
>
> $$
> \mathbb Z^2/\mathbb Z(3,4).
> $$
>
> Since $\gcd(3,4)=1$, the vector $(3,4)$ is primitive. For example, $(3,4)$ and $(-1,-1)$ form a basis of $\mathbb Z^2$ because their determinant is $1$. Quotienting by the first basis vector leaves the infinite cyclic group generated by the residue of the second. Thus
>
> $$
> \boxed{\mathbb Z^2/\mathbb Z(3,4)\cong\mathbb Z.}
> $$
