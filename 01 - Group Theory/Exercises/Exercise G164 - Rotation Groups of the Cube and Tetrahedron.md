# Exercise G164: Rotation Groups of the Cube and Tetrahedron

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 9.1
> Use the counting formula to determine the orders of the groups of rotational symmetries of a cube and of a tetrahedron.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Let each rotation group act transitively on the vertices.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> A vertex stabilizer consists of rotations about the axis through that vertex and the center.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> For a cube, the orbit of a vertex has size $8$. Its stabilizer consists of the identity and the two rotations through $\pm120^\circ$ about the body diagonal through that vertex, so it has order $3$. Hence
>
> $$
> |O|=8\cdot3=24.
> $$
>
> For a regular tetrahedron, the vertex orbit has size $4$, and the stabilizer again has order $3$, generated by the $120^\circ$ rotation about the vertex and the center of the opposite face. Therefore
>
> $$
> |T|=4\cdot3=12.
> $$
>
> $\square$

- These are orientation-preserving groups; allowing orientation reversal doubles both orders.
