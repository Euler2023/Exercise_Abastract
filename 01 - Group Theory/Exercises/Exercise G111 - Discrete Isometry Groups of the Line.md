# Exercise G111: Discrete Isometry Groups of the Line

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.7
> Let $N$ denote the group of isometries of a line $\mathbb R^1$. Classify discrete subgroups of $N$, identifying those that differ in the choice of origin and unit length on the line.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Every line isometry is either $t_a(x)=x+a$ or $t_as(x)=a-x$, where $s(x)=-x$.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> First classify the translation subgroup. If a reflection is present, move the origin to its fixed point.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Every isometry of the line has the form $x\mapsto x+a$ or $x\mapsto -x+a$. Let $H\le N$ be discrete, and let $T$ be its subgroup of translations. The set
>
> $$
> A=\{a\in\mathbb R:t_a\in H\}
> $$
>
> is a discrete additive subgroup of $\mathbb R$, so either $A=\{0\}$ or $A=c\mathbb Z$ for a unique $c>0$.
>
> If $H$ has no orientation-reversing element, then $H=T$ and is either trivial or infinite cyclic. If $H$ contains a reflection $u(x)=a-x$, change the origin to its fixed point $a/2$; then $u$ becomes $s(x)=-x$. Any other reflection $v$ satisfies that $vs$ is a translation, so $H$ is generated by $T$ and $s$.
>
> Thus, up to a change of origin and unit length, the complete list is
>
> $$
> \{1\},\qquad
> \langle s\rangle\cong C_2,\qquad
> \langle t_1\rangle\cong C_\infty,\qquad
> \langle t_1,s\mid s^2=1,\ st_1s=t_1^{-1}\rangle\cong D_\infty.
> $$
>
> All four groups are discrete, so the list is exact. $\square$
