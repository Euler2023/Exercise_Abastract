# Exercise G325: Generating a Group from Overlapping Translates

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 57
> Let $G$ act on $X$ and let $Y\subseteq X$. Put
>
> $$
> G_Y=\{g\in G:gY\cap Y\ne\varnothing\},
> $$
>
> and let $\overline G_Y$ be the subgroup generated by $G_Y$. Prove that $\overline G_YY$ and $(G\setminus\overline G_Y)Y$ are disjoint. Deduce that if $X=GY$ and such a disjoint decomposition cannot have two nonempty parts, then $G_Y$ generates $G$. Apply this when $X$ is connected and translates of $Y$ are all open, or when the action is discrete and unions of translates of the closed set $Y$ are closed.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> An equality $g_1y_1=g_2y_2$ forces $g_2^{-1}g_1\in G_Y$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Suppose, contrary to the first assertion, that $g_1y_1=g_2y_2$ with $g_1\in\overline G_Y$, $g_2\notin\overline G_Y$, and $y_1,y_2\in Y$. Then
>
> $$
> (g_2^{-1}g_1)y_1=y_2,
> $$
>
> so $(g_2^{-1}g_1)Y\cap Y\ne\varnothing$ and $g_2^{-1}g_1\in G_Y\subseteq\overline G_Y$. Hence $g_2=g_1(g_2^{-1}g_1)^{-1}\in\overline G_Y$, a contradiction. The two unions are disjoint.
>
> If $X=GY$, these two unions cover $X$. The first is nonempty when $Y$ is nonempty. Under the stated indecomposability assumption, the second must therefore be empty, so $G=\overline G_Y$ and $G_Y$ generates $G$.
>
> If $X$ is connected, $Y$ is open, and $G$ acts by homeomorphisms, both unions are open; a disjoint open cover has an empty part. In the discrete-action variant with $Y$ closed, the assumed discreteness makes arbitrary relevant unions of translates closed, so the two parts are also complementary open sets; connectedness again forces one part to be empty. Thus $G_Y$ generates $G$ in both examples.
