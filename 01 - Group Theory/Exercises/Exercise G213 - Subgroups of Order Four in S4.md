# Exercise G213: Subgroups of Order Four in S4

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.6
> Find all subgroups of $S_4$ of order $4$, and decide which are normal.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> A group of order $4$ is cyclic or Klein four.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The three cyclic groups are generated by representatives $(1234),(1243),(1324)$. The Klein groups are
>
> $$
> V=\{1,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}
> $$
>
> and $\langle(12),(34)\rangle$, $\langle(13),(24)\rangle$, $\langle(14),(23)\rangle$. This exhausts the cases because every group of order $4$ is $C_4$ or $C_2^2$. Conjugation permutes the three cyclic groups and the last three Klein groups. Only $V$, the union of the identity class and the double-transposition class, is normal. $\square$
