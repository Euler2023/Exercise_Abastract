# Exercise G224: Extending Sylow Subgroups Along a Group Inclusion

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 7.2
> Let $G_1\subset G_2$, and let $H_1$ be a Sylow $p$-subgroup of $G_1$. Prove that some Sylow $p$-subgroup $H_2$ of $G_2$ satisfies $H_1=H_2\cap G_1$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Put $H_1$ inside a Sylow subgroup of $G_2$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> By Sylow I–II, the $p$-subgroup $H_1$ is contained in some Sylow $p$-subgroup $H_2$ of $G_2$. Then $H_2\cap G_1$ is a $p$-subgroup of $G_1$ containing $H_1$. Maximality of the Sylow subgroup $H_1$ forces equality. $\square$
