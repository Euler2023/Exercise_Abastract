# Exercise G27: A $p$-Cycle and a Transposition Generate $S_p$

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 12.8
> Let $p$ be prime. Prove that $S_p$ is generated by any $p$-cycle together with any transposition.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Label the cycle by $\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$ and conjugate the transposition repeatedly.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Relabel so the $p$-cycle is $c:x\mapsto x+1$ on $\mathbb F_p$, and let the transposition be $\tau=(a\,b)$, $a\ne b$. Its conjugates are
> $$
> c^k\tau c^{-k}=(a+k\,\,b+k).
> $$
> These are the edges of the graph on $\mathbb F_p$ joining $x$ to $x+d$, where $d=b-a\ne0$. Since $p$ is prime, $d$ generates the additive group, so this graph is connected. Transpositions along the edges of any connected graph generate the full symmetric group. Hence $\langle c,\tau\rangle=S_p$.
