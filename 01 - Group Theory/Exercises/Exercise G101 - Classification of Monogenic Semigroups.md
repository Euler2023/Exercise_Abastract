# Exercise G101: Classification of Monogenic Semigroups

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise M.4
> A semigroup $S$ is a set with an associative law of composition and with an identity. Elements are not required to have inverses, and the Cancellation Law need not hold. A semigroup $S$ is generated by $s$ if $S=\{1,s,s^2,\ldots\}$. Classify semigroups generated by one element.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> If all powers are distinct, the answer is immediate.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> Otherwise choose the first repetition $s^{m+r}=s^m$ with $m$ minimal and then $r$ minimal.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> If the powers $1,s,s^2,\ldots$ are all distinct, the semigroup is the free monogenic monoid $\mathbb N_0$ under addition of exponents.
> 
> Otherwise there are unique integers $m\ge0$ and $r\ge1$, chosen with $m$ minimal and then $r$ minimal, such that
> 
> $$
> s^{m+r}=s^m.
> $$
> 
> Then
> 
> $$
> S=\{1,s,\ldots,s^{m+r-1}\},
> $$
> 
> these elements are distinct, and every exponent $n\ge m$ is reduced by repeatedly replacing $n$ with $n-r$. Thus multiplication is addition of exponents followed, once the exponent reaches $m$, by reduction modulo $r$. Conversely every pair $(m,r)$ with $m\ge0$, $r\ge1$ defines such an associative monoid. These pairs, together with the infinite case, give the classification.
