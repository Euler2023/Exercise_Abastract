# Exercise G53: Products $ab$ and $ba$ Are Conjugate

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 6.4
> Prove that $ab$ and $ba$ are conjugate elements of a group.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Conjugate by $b$ or by $a^{-1}$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> We have
> 
> $$
> b(ab)b^{-1}=ba.
> $$
> 
> Thus $ba$ is conjugate to $ab$. This strengthens Exercise 4.3: conjugate elements have the same order.
