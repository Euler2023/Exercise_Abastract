# Exercise G212: Products pq and qp Have Equal Cycle Sizes

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.5
> Let $p,q$ be permutations. Prove that $pq$ and $qp$ have cycles of equal sizes.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Show that the two products are conjugate.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The identity $q(pq)q^{-1}=qp$ shows that the products are conjugate. Conjugation only relabels entries in the disjoint cycles, so their cycle sizes agree. $\square$
