# Exercise G279: Surjectivity of Reduction from SL2(Z) to SL2(Fp)

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Miscellaneous Exercise *M.7
> Prove that the homomorphism $SL_2(\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow SL_2(\mathbb F_p)$ obtained by reducing the matrix entries modulo $p$ is surjective.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Show that $SL_2(\mathbb F_p)$ is generated by the elementary matrices $E_{12}(t)$ and $E_{21}(t)$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> For $t\in\mathbb F_p$, put
>
> $$
> E_{12}(t)=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
> \qquad
> E_{21}(t)=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\t&1\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> These matrices lift to the same elementary matrices with any integer representative of $t$. It therefore suffices to prove that they generate $SL_2(\mathbb F_p)$.
>
> Define, for $u\ne0$,
>
> $$
> w(u)=E_{12}(u)E_{21}(-u^{-1})E_{12}(u)
> =\begin{pmatrix}0&u\\-u^{-1}&0\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> w(u)w(1)^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}u&0\\0&u^{-1}\end{pmatrix},
> $$
>
> so every determinant-one diagonal matrix is elementary-generated.
>
> Now let $A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in SL_2(\mathbb F_p)$. If $a=0$, multiplication by $w(1)$ interchanges the rows up to sign and makes the upper-left entry nonzero. If $a\ne0$, multiplication by $E_{21}(-ca^{-1})$ eliminates $c$ and leaves an upper-triangular matrix
>
> $$
> \begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&a^{-1}\end{pmatrix}
> =\begin{pmatrix}a&0\\0&a^{-1}\end{pmatrix}E_{12}(b/a).
> $$
>
> Hence every element of $SL_2(\mathbb F_p)$ is a product of elementary matrices, all of which lift to $SL_2(\mathbb Z)$. The reduction homomorphism is surjective.
