# Exercise G41: Involutions in $S_4$

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 4.9
> How many elements of order $2$ does $S_4$ contain?

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> An involution is a product of disjoint transpositions.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> There are $\binom42=6$ single transpositions. A product of two disjoint transpositions corresponds to a partition of four letters into two unordered pairs; there are
> 
> $$
> \frac{1}{2}\binom42=3
> $$
> 
> such elements. Therefore $S_4$ has
> 
> $$
> 6+3=9
> $$
> 
> elements of order $2$.
