# Exercise G35: Products $ab$ and $ba$ Have the Same Order

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 4.3
> Let $a,b\in G$. Prove that $ab$ and $ba$ have the same order.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Show the two elements are conjugate.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Conjugating $ab$ by $b$ gives
> 
> $$
> b(ab)b^{-1}=ba.
> $$
> 
> Conjugation preserves all powers and hence preserves order. Therefore $ab$ and $ba$ have the same order, finite or infinite.
