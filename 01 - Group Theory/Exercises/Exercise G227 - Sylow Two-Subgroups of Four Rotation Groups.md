# Exercise G227: Sylow Two-Subgroups of Four Rotation Groups

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 7.5
> Find Sylow $2$-subgroups of **(a)** $D_{10}$, **(b)** $T$, **(c)** $O$, **(d)** $I$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Use $T\cong A_4$, $O\cong S_4$, $I\cong A_5$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> With Artin's convention $|D_{10}|=20$, a Sylow $2$-subgroup is $\langle r^5,s\rangle\cong V_4$. In $T\cong A_4$, it is the normal Klein four group. In $O\cong S_4$, it is a dihedral group of order $8$, for example $\langle(1234),(13)\rangle$. In $I\cong A_5$, it is any Klein four group consisting of the identity and three double transpositions. $\square$
