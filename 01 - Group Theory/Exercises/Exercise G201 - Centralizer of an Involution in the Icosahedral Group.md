# Exercise G201: Centralizer of an Involution in the Icosahedral Group

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 4.3
> What is the centralizer of an element of order $2$ of the icosahedral group $I$?

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Use the conjugacy class of the fifteen half-turns.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The group $I\cong A_5$ has $15$ elements of order $2$, all conjugate (the double transpositions). Hence an involution $x$ has centralizer of order $60/15=4$. The identity, $x$, and the other two half-turns about axes perpendicular to the same inscribed rectangular configuration form a Klein four group. Thus
>
> $$
> Z_I(x)\cong C_2\times C_2.
> $$
>
> $\square$
