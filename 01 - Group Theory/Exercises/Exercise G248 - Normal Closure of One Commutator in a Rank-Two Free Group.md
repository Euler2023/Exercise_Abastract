# Exercise G248: Normal Closure of One Commutator in a Rank-Two Free Group

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 10.10
> Let $F=F(x,y)$ and let $R$ be the smallest normal subgroup containing $xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$. **(a)** Show $x^2y^2x^{-2}y^{-2}\in R$. **(b)** Prove $R=[F,F]$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The quotient $F/R$ is generated by two commuting elements.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> In $F/R$, the images of $x,y$ commute. Hence $x^2y^2x^{-2}y^{-2}$ maps to $1$, proving **(a)**. The quotient $F/R$ is abelian, so the minimality property of the commutator subgroup gives $[F,F]\subseteq R$. Conversely $[F,F]$ is normal and contains the relator $[x,y]$, so minimality of the normal closure gives $R\subseteq[F,F]$. Therefore $R=[F,F]$. $\square$
