# Exercise G64: Geometric Meaning of Reflexive and Symmetric Relations

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 7.4
> View a relation on $\mathbb R$ as a subset of the $(x,y)$-plane. Explain geometrically the reflexive and symmetric properties.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The diagonal represents pairs $(x,x)$; swapping coordinates reflects across it.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Reflexivity means that every point $(x,x)$ lies in the relation, so the entire diagonal line $y=x$ is contained in the locus.
> 
> Symmetry means that whenever $(x,y)$ is in the locus, so is $(y,x)$. Geometrically, the locus is invariant under reflection across the line $y=x$.
