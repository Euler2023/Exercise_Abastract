# Exercise G28: Products of Permutations as Disjoint Cycles

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.1
> Write the following products as products of disjoint cycles:
> 
> $$
> (12)(13)(14)(15),\quad (123)(234)(345),\quad
> (1234)(2345),\quad (12)(23)(34)(45)(51).
> $$

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Apply the rightmost cycle first, following Artin's function-composition convention.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Following each index through the factors from right to left gives
> 
> $$
> (12)(13)(14)(15)=(15432),
> $$
> 
> $$
> (123)(234)(345)=(12)(45),
> $$
> 
> $$
> (1234)(2345)=(12453),
> $$
> 
> and
> 
> $$
> (12)(23)(34)(45)(51)=(2345),
> $$
> 
> with omitted indices fixed.
