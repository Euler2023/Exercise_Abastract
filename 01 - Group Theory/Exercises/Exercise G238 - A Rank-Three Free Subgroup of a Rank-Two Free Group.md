# Exercise G238: A Rank-Three Free Subgroup of a Rank-Two Free Group

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 9.1
> Let $F=F(x,y)$. Prove that $u=x^2$, $v=y$, and $z=xy$ generate a subgroup freely generated by $u,v,z$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Build the folded labeled graph carrying the three loops.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Construct a labeled graph with two vertices whose based loops read $x^2$, $y$, and $xy$. At each vertex there is at most one outgoing edge with any label $x^{\pm1},y^{\pm1}$, so the graph maps immersively to the rose with loops $x,y$. An immersion of graphs induces an injection on fundamental groups. The graph has first Betti number $E-V+1=3$, and the three displayed loops form a spanning-tree basis. Hence their images $u,v,z$ freely generate the subgroup they span in $F(x,y)$. $\square$
