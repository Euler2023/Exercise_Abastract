# Exercise G272: Four Abelian Groups from Relations

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 7.3
> Find an isomorphic direct product of cyclic groups when $V$ is the abelian group generated by $x,y,z$, with the given relations.
>
> **(a)** $3x+2y+8z=0$, $2x+4z=0$.
>
> **(b)** $x+y=0$, $2x=0$, $4x+2z=0$, $4x+2y+2z=0$.
>
> **(c)** $2x+y=0$, $x-y+3z=0$.
>
> **(d)** $7x+5y+2z=0$, $3x+3y=0$, $13x+11y+2z=0$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Put the coefficient vectors of the relations into a matrix and compute its Smith form.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Exact Smith reduction of the four relation matrices gives
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{c|c|c}
> \text{case}&\text{nonzero Smith entries}&V\\ \hline
> (a)&1,4&\mathbb Z\oplus C_4\\
> (b)&1,2,2&C_2\oplus C_2\\
> (c)&1,3&\mathbb Z\oplus C_3\\
> (d)&1,6&\mathbb Z\oplus C_6.
> \end{array}
> $$
>
> For example, in (a) the relation matrix has rank two, gcd of entries $1$, and gcd of its $2\times2$ minors $4$; hence its quotient has one free generator and one cyclic torsion factor of order four. The same determinantal-divisor calculation yields the remaining rows. Therefore
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \begin{aligned}
> (a)&\ \mathbb Z\oplus C_4,&
> (b)&\ C_2\oplus C_2,\\
> (c)&\ \mathbb Z\oplus C_3,&
> (d)&\ \mathbb Z\oplus C_6.
> \end{aligned}}
> $$
