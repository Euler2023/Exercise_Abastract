# Exercise G105: The Homophonic Group Is Trivial

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise M.16
> The homophonic group $\mathcal H$ is generated by the letters of the alphabet, subject to the relations that English words with the same dictionary pronunciation represent equal group elements. Thus $be=bee$, and cancellation gives $e=1$. Determine $\mathcal H$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Use homophone pairs to eliminate letters successively.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> After eliminating the vowels, pairs such as *sow/so*, *hour/our*, and *knight/night* eliminate consonants.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The group is trivial. Here is an explicit elimination chain; in each equality, letters already shown trivial are cancelled.
> 
> First *bye/by* gives $e=1$. Then *lead/led*, *maid/made*, *sow/sew*, *buy/by*, and *lye/lie* give
> 
> $$
> a=i=o=u=y=1.
> $$
> 
> Next *sow/so*, *hour/our*, *knight/night*, *damn/dam*, *psalter/salter*, and *plumb/plum* give
> 
> $$
> w=h=k=n=p=b=1.
> $$
> 
> The pairs *base/bass*, *butt/but*, *tolled/told*, *barred/bard*, and *dammed/damned* then give
> 
> $$
> s=t=l=r=m=1.
> $$
> 
> The pairs *chased/chaste* and *sign/sine* give $d=g=1$. Finally *daze/days*, *cite/sight*, *jeans/genes*, *queue/cue*, *tax/tacks*, *phase/faze*, and *chivvy/chivy* give
> 
> $$
> z=c=j=q=x=f=v=1.
> $$
> 
> Thus all twenty-six generators are the identity, so $\mathcal H=\{1\}$.
