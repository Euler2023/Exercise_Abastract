# Exercise LA470: Positive Chambers and Trace Dual Cones

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Lang XV.28 — Positivity
> On $\mathfrak a$ (real diagonal matrices with trace $0$) the form of Exercise 27 can be defined by $\operatorname{tr}(XY)$, since elements $X,Y\in\mathfrak a$ are symmetric. Let $\mathcal A=\{\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_{n-1}\}$ denote the basis of Exercise 26. Define an element $H\in\mathfrak a$ to be semipositive (written $H\ge0$) if $\alpha_i(H)\ge0$ for all $i=1,\ldots,n-1$. For each $\alpha\in\mathfrak a^\vee$, let $H_\alpha$ represent $\alpha$ with respect to $B_t$, that is $\langle H_\alpha,H\rangle=\alpha(H)$ for all $H\in\mathfrak a$. Show that $H\ge0$ if and only if
> $$
> H=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}s_iH_{\alpha_i'}\qquad\text{with }s_i\ge0.
> $$
> Similarly, define $H$ to be positive and formulate the similar condition with $s_i>0$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The trace-pairing representative of $\alpha_i$ is $H_i=\operatorname{diag}(0,\ldots,1,-1,\ldots,0)$.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> Symmetry of the pairing gives $\alpha_j(H_{\alpha_i'})=\alpha_i'(H_j)=\delta_{ij}$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Independent derivation
> Use the notation of [[04 - Linear Algebra and Modules/Exercises/Exercise LA468 - Coordinate Differences and Dual Bases on Trace Zero Diagonals|Exercise LA468]]. The restriction of $B_t$ is positive definite by [[04 - Linear Algebra and Modules/Exercises/Exercise LA469 - The Positive Definite Trace Pairing|Exercise LA469]]. Its map $\mathfrak a\to\mathfrak a^\vee$ is injective and hence, by equal dimensions, an isomorphism. This gives existence and uniqueness of every representative $H_\alpha$.
>
> Since $\operatorname{tr}(H_jH)=h_j-h_{j+1}=\alpha_j(H)$, we have $H_{\alpha_j}=H_j$. Put $W_i=H_{\alpha_i'}$. Then
> $$
> \alpha_j(W_i)=B_t(H_j,W_i)=B_t(W_i,H_j)=\alpha_i'(H_j)=\delta_{ij}.
> $$
> Thus the $W_i$ form the basis dual to the functionals $\alpha_j$, and every $H\in\mathfrak a$ has the unique expansion
> $$
> H=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\alpha_i(H)W_i.
> $$
> Consequently all $\alpha_i(H)$ are nonnegative if and only if all the coefficients $s_i$ are nonnegative. Defining positive by $\alpha_i(H)>0$ for every $i$ gives exactly the condition $s_i>0$ for every $i$.
>
> For an explicit check, the representatives are
> $$
> W_i=\operatorname{diag}\left(
> \underbrace{\frac{n-i}{n},\ldots,\frac{n-i}{n}}_{i},
> \underbrace{-\frac{i}{n},\ldots,-\frac{i}{n}}_{n-i}\right).
> $$
> They have trace zero, and pairing with a trace-zero diagonal $H$ yields $\sum_{j=1}^i h_j=\alpha_i'(H)$.

## Notes

- Source: [S2, Ch. XV, Exercise 28, printed p. 600, PDF p. 615], visually verified. The solution independently uses Exercises 26 and 27.
- Here semipositivity means $h_1\ge\cdots\ge h_n$, and positivity means strict consecutive inequalities. These terms refer to a chamber, not to positive semidefinite or positive definite matrices. A positive semidefinite trace-zero matrix must be zero.
- The cone is generated by the trace representatives of the primed functionals, not by the $H_i$. For $n=1$, the zero-dimensional assertions are interpreted with empty sums and vacuous inequalities.
