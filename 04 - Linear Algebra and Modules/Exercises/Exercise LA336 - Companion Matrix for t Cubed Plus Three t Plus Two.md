# Exercise LA336: Companion Matrix for t^3 + 3t + 2

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 8.3 — printed statement
> Let $R=F[x]$ be the polynomial ring in one variable over a field $F$, and let $V$ be the $R$-module generated by an element $v$ that satisfies the relation $(t^3+3t+2)v=0$. Choose a basis for $V$ as an $F$-vector space, and determine the matrix of multiplication by $t$ with respect to this basis.

> [!warning] Source notation and presentation issue
> The ring variable is first called $x$ and then $t$; the intended ring is $F[t]$. Also, merely “satisfies” the displayed relation would allow additional relations and would not determine $V$. The computation assumes the intended one-relation presentation $V=F[t]v/((t^3+3t+2)v)$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Use $v,tv,t^2v$ and replace $t^3v$ with $-3tv-2v$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution of the intended presentation
> Since the defining polynomial is monic of degree three,
>
> $$
> \mathcal B=(v,tv,t^2v)
> $$
>
> is an $F$-basis. Multiplication by $t$ gives
>
> $$
> t(v)=tv,
> \qquad
> t(tv)=t^2v,
> \qquad
> t(t^2v)=-2v-3tv.
> $$
>
> Thus, with column-coordinate convention, the matrix is
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 0&0&-2\\
> 1&0&-3\\
> 0&1&0
> \end{pmatrix}.}
> $$
>
> The entries $-2$ and $-3$ are interpreted in $F$, so they reduce appropriately in characteristics $2$ or $3$.
