# Exercise LA71: Generators of $SL_2(\mathbb Z)$

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise M.14
> Prove that
> 
> $$
> E=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> E'=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix}
> $$
> 
> generate $SL_2(\mathbb Z)$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Use powers of $E$ and $E'$ as integer row additions.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> Apply the Euclidean algorithm to the first column rather than guessing a word directly.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Left multiplication by $E^q$ adds $q$ times the second row to the first; left multiplication by $(E')^q$ adds $q$ times the first row to the second. For
> 
> $$
> A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in SL_2(\mathbb Z),
> $$
> 
> we have $\gcd(a,c)=1$. The Euclidean algorithm, implemented by these integer row additions, reduces the first column to $(\pm1,0)^t$. The matrix
> 
> $$
> E(E')^{-1}E=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}
> $$
> 
> and its powers supply row interchange together with the needed sign change, so the first column may be reduced to $(1,0)^t$. The determinant condition then leaves a matrix $\begin{pmatrix}1&m\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=E^m$, which is also generated by $E$. Reversing the row-reduction operations expresses $A$ as a word in $E,E',E^{-1},(E')^{-1}$.
