# Exercise LA334: A Cyclic Module for a Two-Eigenvalue Operator

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 8.1
> Let $T$ be the linear operator on $\mathbb C^2$ whose matrix is
>
> $$
> \begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Is the corresponding $\mathbb C[t]$-module cyclic?

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Test whether $e_2$ and $T(e_2)$ form a vector-space basis.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Let $v=e_2=(0,1)^{\mathsf T}$. Then
>
> $$
> T(v)=(1,1)^{\mathsf T}.
> $$
>
> The two vectors $v,T(v)$ are linearly independent, so they span $\mathbb C^2$. Since polynomial multiples of $v$ contain both $v$ and $tv=T(v)$, the $\mathbb C[t]$-submodule generated by $v$ is the whole space. Therefore
>
> $$
> \boxed{V\text{ is cyclic, generated by }e_2.}
> $$
>
> Its annihilator is generated by the minimal polynomial
>
> $$
> (t-1)(t-2),
> $$
>
> so $V\cong\mathbb C[t]/((t-1)(t-2))$.
