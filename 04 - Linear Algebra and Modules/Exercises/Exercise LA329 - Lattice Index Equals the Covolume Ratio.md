# Exercise LA329: Lattice Index Equals the Covolume Ratio

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 4.9
> Use diagonalization to prove that if $L\subset M$ are lattices, then
>
> $$
> [M:L]=\frac{\Delta(L)}{\Delta(M)}.
> $$

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Represent a basis of $L$ in a basis of $M$, and put the resulting integer matrix into Smith form.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Choose a basis $C$ of $M$ and a basis $B$ of $L$. Since $L\subset M$, there is a nonsingular integer matrix $A$ with
>
> $$
> B=CA.
> $$
>
> Choose unimodular $P,Q$ so that
>
> $$
> Q^{-1}AP=D=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,d_2).
> $$
>
> The matrices $P$ and $Q$ merely replace the chosen lattice bases and have determinants $\pm1$, so neither index nor fundamental area changes. In the diagonal bases, $L$ is generated by $d_1c_1$ and $d_2c_2$. Therefore
>
> $$
> [M:L]=d_1d_2
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \Delta(L)=d_1d_2\Delta(M).
> $$
>
> Dividing gives the required formula.
