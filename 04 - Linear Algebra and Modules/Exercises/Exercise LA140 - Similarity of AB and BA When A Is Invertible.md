# Exercise LA140: Similarity of AB and BA When A Is Invertible

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 6.7
> If $A,B$ are $n\times n$ and $A$ is nonsingular, prove that $AB$ is similar to $BA$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Conjugate $AB$ by $A$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Directly,
>
> $$
> A^{-1}(AB)A=BA.
> $$
>
> Since $A$ is invertible, this is a similarity transformation.
