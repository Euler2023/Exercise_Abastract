# Exercise LA571: An Integral Basis at Almost All Completions

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Neukirch VI.2.1
> Let $\omega_1,\ldots,\omega_n$ be a basis of $L|K$. Then the isomorphism $L\otimes_KK_{\mathfrak p}\simeq\prod_{\mathfrak P\mid\mathfrak p}L_{\mathfrak P}$ induces, for almost all prime ideals $\mathfrak p$ of $K$, an isomorphism
> $$
> \omega_1\mathfrak o_{\mathfrak p}\oplus\cdots\oplus\omega_n\mathfrak o_{\mathfrak p}\simeq\prod_{\mathfrak P\mid\mathfrak p}\mathcal O_{\mathfrak P},
> $$
> where $\mathfrak o_{\mathfrak p}$, resp. $\mathcal O_{\mathfrak P}$, is the valuation ring of $K_{\mathfrak p}$, resp. $L_{\mathfrak P}$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint
> Compare the lattice generated by the basis with $\mathcal O_L$ after clearing denominators. The discrepancy is supported at only finitely many primes.

> [!hint]- Stronger hint
> Find d nonzero with d O_L contained in the chosen basis lattice and that lattice contained in d^{-1} O_L; invert d and complete.

## Solution

> [!success]- Independent derivation
> Write $M=\sum_i\mathcal O_K\omega_i\subset L$. Both it and $\mathcal O_L$ are full $\mathcal O_K$-lattices, so there is a nonzero $d\in\mathcal O_K$ such that $d\mathcal O_L\subseteq M\subseteq d^{-1}\mathcal O_L$. Localizing and completing at $\mathfrak p\nmid d$ gives
> $$
> M\otimes_{\mathcal O_K}\mathcal O_\mathfrak p
> =\mathcal O_L\otimes_{\mathcal O_K}\mathcal O_\mathfrak p.
> $$
> The right side is isomorphic to $\prod_{\mathfrak P\mid\mathfrak p}\mathcal O_\mathfrak P$: the $\mathfrak p$-adic completion of a finite $\mathcal O_K$-module decomposes into the relatively prime $\mathfrak P$-adic components by taking inverse limits in the Chinese remainder theorem. Since $\omega_i$ is a $K$-basis, the left side is exactly the direct sum in the statement. Thus the assertion holds outside finitely many $\mathfrak p\mid d$.
