# Exercise LA19: Reading Matrix Entries

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 1.1
> What are the entries $a_{21}$ and $a_{23}$ of the matrix
> 
> $$
> A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&5\\2&7&8\\0&9&4\end{bmatrix}?
> $$

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The first subscript is the row and the second is the column.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> The entry in row $2$, column $1$ is $2$, while the entry in row $2$, column $3$ is $8$. Hence
> 
> $$
> a_{21}=2,\qquad a_{23}=8.
> $$
