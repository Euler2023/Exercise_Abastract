# Exercise LA194: Discrete Subgroups of $\mathbb R^3$

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.12
> Classify discrete subgroups of the additive group $\mathbb R^{3+}$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Let $V=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}(\Gamma)$ and choose a maximal linearly independent family from $\Gamma$.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> The fundamental parallelepiped of the lattice generated by that family contains only finitely many elements of $\Gamma$ modulo the smaller lattice.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Let $\Gamma\le\mathbb R^3$ be discrete and put $V=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}(\Gamma)$, with $r=\dim V\le3$. Choose $\mathbb R$-linearly independent elements $v_1,\ldots,v_r\in\Gamma$ spanning $V$, and set
>
> $$
> L_0=\mathbb Zv_1+\cdots+\mathbb Zv_r.
> $$
>
> A fundamental parallelepiped for $L_0$ is compact. Since $\Gamma$ is a discrete subgroup, distinct elements of $\Gamma$ are uniformly separated near $0$; therefore its intersection with a compact set is finite. Every coset in $\Gamma/L_0$ has a representative in the closed fundamental parallelepiped, so $\Gamma/L_0$ is finite. Hence $\Gamma$ is finitely generated.
>
> The additive group $\Gamma$ is torsion-free. By the structure theorem for finitely generated abelian groups, $\Gamma\cong\mathbb Z^s$ for some $s$. Tensoring its inclusion in $V$ with $\mathbb R$ shows $s=\dim V=r$. Consequently, $\Gamma$ has a $\mathbb Z$-basis $w_1,\ldots,w_r$ that is linearly independent over $\mathbb R$, and
>
> $$
> \Gamma=\mathbb Zw_1\oplus\cdots\oplus\mathbb Zw_r,
> \qquad 0\le r\le3.
> $$
>
> Conversely, the integer span of any $r$ linearly independent vectors in $\mathbb R^3$ is discrete. Thus the possibilities are: the trivial group; a rank-one lattice on a line; a rank-two lattice in a plane; or a full rank-three lattice in $\mathbb R^3$. $\square$
