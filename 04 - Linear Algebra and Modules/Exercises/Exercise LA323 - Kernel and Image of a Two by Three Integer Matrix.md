# Exercise LA323: Kernel and Image of a Two-by-Three Integer Matrix

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 4.3
> Determine all integer solutions to the system $AX=0$, when
>
> $$
> A=\begin{pmatrix}4&7&2\\2&4&6\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Find a basis for the space of integer column vectors $B$ such that $AX=B$ has a solution.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Use the explicit matrix $P$ from Exercise 4.1(c), for which $AP=(I_1\oplus[2]\oplus[0])$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> From Exercise 4.1(c),
>
> $$
> AP=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&2&0\end{pmatrix},
> \qquad
> P=\begin{pmatrix}2&-7&17\\-1&4&-10\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> The kernel of the diagonal matrix is generated by $e_3$, so the integer kernel of $A$ is generated by the third column of $P$:
>
> $$
> \boxed{X=t(17,-10,1)^{\mathsf T},\qquad t\in\mathbb Z.}
> $$
>
> The image of the diagonal matrix, and hence of $A$, is
>
> $$
> \mathbb Z(1,0)^{\mathsf T}\oplus\mathbb Z(0,2)^{\mathsf T}.
> $$
>
> Thus $AX=B$ is solvable exactly for $B=(u,2v)^{\mathsf T}$, and a requested basis is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(1,0)^{\mathsf T},\ (0,2)^{\mathsf T}.}
> $$

- **Routing:** Linear Algebra is primary because Smith form transports bases of the kernel and image.
- **Computational verification:** Direct multiplication gives $A(17,-10,1)^{\mathsf T}=0$, $A(2,-1,0)^{\mathsf T}=(1,0)^{\mathsf T}$, and $A(-7,4,0)^{\mathsf T}=(0,2)^{\mathsf T}$.
