# Exercise LA331: A Gaussian Module from Two Relations

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 7.7
> Let $R=\mathbb Z[i]$ and let $V$ be the $R$-module generated by $v_1,v_2$ with relations
>
> $$
> (1+i)v_1+(2-i)v_2=0,
> \qquad
> 3v_1+5iv_2=0.
> $$
>
> Write this module as a direct sum of cyclic modules.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Compute the Smith invariants of the relation matrix over the Euclidean domain $\mathbb Z[i]$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> A presentation matrix is
>
> $$
> A=\begin{pmatrix}1+i&3\\2-i&5i\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> The four entries have Gaussian gcd $1$: any common divisor of $1+i$ and $3$ has norm dividing both $2$ and $9$, hence is a unit. Thus the first invariant factor is $1$. The determinant is
>
> $$
> \det A=(1+i)5i-3(2-i)=-11+8i.
> $$
>
> Hence the Smith form is associated to $\operatorname{diag}(1,-11+8i)$, and
>
> $$
> \boxed{V\cong R/(-11+8i).}
> $$
>
> If one keeps the trivial cyclic summand, this is $R/(1)\oplus R/(-11+8i)$.
