# Exercise LA322: The First Smith Invariant Is the Entry GCD

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 4.2
> Let $d_1,d_2,\ldots$ be the integers referred to in Theorem 14.4.6. Prove that $d_1$ is the greatest common divisor of the entries $a_{ij}$ of $A$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Show that the ideal generated by all entries is unchanged by an elementary row or column operation.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Let $I(A)\subseteq\mathbb Z$ be the ideal generated by all entries of $A$. An elementary row or column operation replaces entries by integer linear combinations of the old entries, so $I(A')\subseteq I(A)$. Applying the inverse elementary operation gives the reverse inclusion. Hence $I(A)$ is invariant under unimodular equivalence.
>
> In Smith form, the nonzero entries are $d_1,\ldots,d_k$ with
>
> $$
> d_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots\mid d_k.
> $$
>
> Therefore the ideal generated by all entries of the Smith form is $(d_1)$. Since this equals the ideal generated by the original $a_{ij}$,
>
> $$
> \boxed{d_1=\gcd(a_{ij})}
> $$
>
> with the convention that $d_1>0$.
