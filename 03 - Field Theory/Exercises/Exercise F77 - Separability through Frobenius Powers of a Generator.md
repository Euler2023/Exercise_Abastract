# Exercise F77: Separability through Frobenius Powers of a Generator

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Lang, Chapter V, Exercise 16
> Let $\operatorname{char}K=p$. Let $\alpha$ be algebraic over $K$. Show that $\alpha$ is separable if and only if $K(\alpha)=K(\alpha^{p^n})$ for all positive integers $n$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> The extension $K(\alpha)/K(\alpha^{p^n})$ is purely inseparable.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> An algebraic element has some Frobenius power separable over its base field.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution and proof status
> Suppose first that $\alpha$ is separable over $K$. Fix $n\ge1$ and put $M=K(\alpha^{p^n})$. The minimal polynomial over $M$ divides the separable minimal polynomial over $K$, so $K(\alpha)/M$ is separable. It is also purely inseparable because $\alpha^{p^n}\in M$. A polynomial which is separable and has only one distinct root must have degree one. Thus $\alpha\in M$, proving equality.
>
> Conversely write the irreducible polynomial of $\alpha$ as
> $$
> m(X)=h(X^{p^e}),
> $$
> where $e\ge0$ is maximal. The polynomial $h$ is irreducible: any factorization of $h$ would induce one of $m$. Its derivative is nonzero by maximality of $e$, so irreducibility implies it is separable. Therefore $\alpha^{p^e}$ is separable over $K$. If $e=0$ we are done; otherwise the assumed equality with $n=e$ gives
> $$
> K(\alpha)=K(\alpha^{p^e}),
> $$
> a separable extension. Hence $\alpha$ is separable.
