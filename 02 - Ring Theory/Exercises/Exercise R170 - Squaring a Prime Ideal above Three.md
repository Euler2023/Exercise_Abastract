# Exercise R170: Squaring a Prime Ideal above Three

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 7.1
> Let $R=\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-5}]$, and let $B=(3,1+\delta)$. Find a generator for the principal ideal $B^2$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Multiply the two lattice generators and compare the result with an element of norm $9$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Here $\delta^2=-5$. The product ideal is generated by
>
> $$
> 9,
> \qquad
> 3+3\delta,
> \qquad
> (1+\delta)^2=-4+2\delta.
> $$
>
> Each generator is divisible by $2-\delta$ in $R$:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> 9&=(2-\delta)(2+\delta),\\
> 3+3\delta&=(2-\delta)(-1+\delta),\\
> -4+2\delta&=(2-\delta)(-2).
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Conversely,
>
> $$
> 2-\delta=-9+(3+3\delta)-2(-4+2\delta),
> $$
>
> so the proposed generator belongs to $B^2$. Therefore
>
> $$
> \boxed{B^2=(2-\delta).}
> $$
