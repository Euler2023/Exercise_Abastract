# Exercise R49: A Parametrized Kernel and Two Generators

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 3.4
> Let $\varphi:\mathbb C[x,y]\to\mathbb C[t]$ send $x\mapsto t+1$ and $y\mapsto t^3-1$. Determine $K=\ker\varphi$, and prove that every ideal $I$ of $\mathbb C[x,y]$ containing $K$ can be generated by two elements.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Eliminate $t$ using $t=x-1$.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> Apply the correspondence theorem and the fact that $\mathbb C[x]$ is a PID.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Since $t=x-1$, the relation between the images is
>
> $$
> y=(x-1)^3-1.
> $$
>
> Hence
>
> $$
> K=\bigl(y-(x-1)^3+1\bigr).
> $$
>
> Indeed, division by this monic linear polynomial in $y$ leaves a remainder $r(x)$, and $r(t+1)=0$ in $\mathbb C[t]$ implies $r=0$.
>
> The quotient $\mathbb C[x,y]/K$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb C[x]$. By the correspondence theorem, $I/K$ is an ideal of $\mathbb C[x]$, hence $(h(x))$ for some $h$. Therefore
>
> $$
> I=\bigl(y-(x-1)^3+1,\ h(x)\bigr),
> $$
>
> so two generators suffice.
