# Exercise R86: Maximal Ideals of the Real Polynomial Ring

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 8.4
> Establish a bijective correspondence between maximal ideals of $\mathbb R[x]$ and points in the upper half plane.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Irreducible real polynomials have degree $1$ or $2$. Choose one root with nonnegative imaginary part.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Since $\mathbb R[x]$ is a PID, its maximal ideals are generated by irreducible polynomials. These are either $x-a$ with $a\in\mathbb R$, or monic irreducible quadratics
>
> $$
> (x-z)(x-\overline z),\qquad \operatorname{Im}z>0.
> $$
>
> Thus assign to $(x-a)$ the boundary point $a$, and to a quadratic the unique root $z$ with positive imaginary part. Conversely, a point $z$ with $\operatorname{Im}z\ge0$ determines the ideal $(x-z)$ when $z$ is real and $((x-z)(x-\overline z))$ otherwise. These constructions are inverse.

- **Source ambiguity:** The printed phrase “upper half plane” must include the real-axis boundary for the stated bijection; with the strict convention $\operatorname{Im}z>0$, the linear maximal ideals would be omitted.
