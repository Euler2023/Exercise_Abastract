# Exercise R187: Prime Ideals in C[x,y] and Z[x]

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Miscellaneous Problem M.3
> Describe the prime ideals in **(a)** the polynomial ring $\mathbb C[x,y]$ in two variables, **(b)** the ring $\mathbb Z[x]$ of integer polynomials.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Both rings are unique factorization domains of Krull dimension two. Height-one primes are generated by irreducible elements.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> Use the Nullstellensatz for maximal ideals of $\mathbb C[x,y]$, and reduce modulo $p$ for maximal ideals of $\mathbb Z[x]$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> **(a)** The prime ideals of $\mathbb C[x,y]$ are exactly
>
> $$
> (0),
> $$
>
> $$
> (f),\qquad f\in\mathbb C[x,y]\text{ irreducible},
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> (x-a,y-b),\qquad (a,b)\in\mathbb C^2.
> $$
>
> The second family consists of the height-one primes because $\mathbb C[x,y]$ is a UFD. Every height-two prime is maximal, and the Hilbert Nullstellensatz identifies every maximal ideal with evaluation at a point $(a,b)$.
>
> **(b)** The prime ideals of $\mathbb Z[x]$ are exactly
>
> $$
> (0),
> $$
>
> the height-one ideals
>
> $$
> (p)\quad(p\text{ a rational prime}),
> $$
>
> $$
> (f)\quad(f\in\mathbb Z[x]\text{ primitive and irreducible of positive degree}),
> $$
>
> and the maximal ideals
>
> $$
> (p,g(x)),
> $$
>
> where $p$ is a rational prime and the reduction $\overline g\in\mathbb F_p[x]$ is irreducible of positive degree. Indeed,
>
> $$
> \mathbb Z[x]/(p,g)\cong\mathbb F_p[x]/(\overline g)
> $$
>
> is a field. Conversely, every maximal ideal of $\mathbb Z[x]$ contains a rational prime $p$; its image in $\mathbb F_p[x]$ is then generated by an irreducible polynomial. The UFD property supplies the remaining height-one primes.
