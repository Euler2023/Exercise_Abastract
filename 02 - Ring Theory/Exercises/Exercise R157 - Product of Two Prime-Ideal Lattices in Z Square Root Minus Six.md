# Exercise R157: Product of Two Prime-Ideal Lattices in Z[sqrt(-6)]

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 4.1
> Let $R=\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-6}]$. Find a lattice basis for the product ideal $AB$, where $A=(2,\delta)$ and $B=(3,\delta)$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Generate $AB$ by the four pairwise products of the displayed generators.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Since $\delta^2=-6$, the product ideal is generated by
>
> $$
> 2\cdot3=6,
> \qquad 2\delta,
> \qquad 3\delta,
> \qquad \delta^2=-6.
> $$
>
> The difference $3\delta-2\delta=delta$ shows that
>
> $$
> AB=(6,\delta).
> $$
>
> Conversely, every one of the four product generators belongs to the lattice generated by $6$ and $\delta$. Hence equality holds. Since $6=-\delta^2$, this is also the principal ideal
>
> $$
> AB=(\delta).
> $$
>
> Thus either ordered pair
>
> $$
> \boxed{(6,\delta)}
> \qquad\text{or}\qquad
> \boxed{(\delta,-6)}
> $$
>
> is a lattice basis for $AB$.
