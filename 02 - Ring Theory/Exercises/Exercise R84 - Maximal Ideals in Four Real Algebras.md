# Exercise R84: Maximal Ideals in Four Real Algebras

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 8.2
> Determine the maximal ideals of: **(a)** $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R$, **(b)** $\mathbb R[x]/(x^2)$, **(c)** $\mathbb R[x]/(x^2-3x+2)$, **(d)** $\mathbb R[x]/(x^2+x+1)$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Factor the defining polynomial and use the quotient-field criterion for maximality.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> **(a)** The maximal ideals are $\mathbb R\times0$ and $0\times\mathbb R$.
>
> **(b)** There is one maximal ideal, generated by the residue $\bar x$; the quotient is $\mathbb R$.
>
> **(c)** Since $x^2-3x+2=(x-1)(x-2)$, the ring is $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R$. Its maximal ideals are generated by the residues of $x-1$ and $x-2$, respectively.
>
> **(d)** The polynomial has negative discriminant and is irreducible over $\mathbb R$, so the quotient is a field isomorphic to $\mathbb C$. Its only maximal ideal is the zero ideal.
