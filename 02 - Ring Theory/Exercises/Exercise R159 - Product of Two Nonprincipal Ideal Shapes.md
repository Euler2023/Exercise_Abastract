# Exercise R159: Product of Two Nonprincipal Ideal Shapes

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 4.3
> Let $R$ be the ring $\mathbb Z[\delta]$, where $\delta=\sqrt{-5}$, and let $A$ and $B$ be ideals of the form
>
> $$
> A=\left(\alpha,\frac12(\alpha+\alpha\delta)\right),
> \qquad
> B=\left(\beta,\frac12(\beta+\beta\delta)\right).
> $$
>
> Prove that $AB$ is a principal ideal by finding a generator.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Put $\gamma=(1+\delta)/2$. As fractional lattices, $A=\alpha(1,\gamma)$ and $B=\beta(1,\gamma)$.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> Show that the product of the fractional lattice $(1,\gamma)$ with itself is $\frac12R$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Set
>
> $$
> \gamma=\frac{1+\delta}{2}.
> $$
>
> Then $A=\alpha(1,\gamma)$ and $B=\beta(1,\gamma)$ as additive lattices. Their product is generated by
>
> $$
> \alpha\beta,
> \qquad
> \alpha\beta\gamma,
> \qquad
> \alpha\beta\gamma^2.
> $$
>
> Since $\delta^2=-5$,
>
> $$
> \gamma^2=\frac{-2+\delta}{2}
> =\gamma-\frac32.
> $$
>
> Thus every displayed generator is a multiple of $\alpha\beta/2$ by an element of $R$, so
>
> $$
> AB\subseteq\left(\frac{\alpha\beta}{2}\right).
> $$
>
> Conversely,
>
> $$
> \frac12=\gamma-\gamma^2-1,
> $$
>
> and hence
>
> $$
> \frac{\alpha\beta}{2}
> =\alpha\beta\gamma-\alpha\beta\gamma^2-\alpha\beta
> \in AB.
> $$
>
> Therefore
>
> $$
> \boxed{AB=\left(\frac{\alpha\beta}{2}\right)}.
> $$
>
> The generator belongs to $R$: Exercise 3.1's parity condition puts both $\alpha$ and $\beta$ in the ideal $(2,1+\delta)$, whose square is $(2)$, so $\alpha\beta\in2R$.
