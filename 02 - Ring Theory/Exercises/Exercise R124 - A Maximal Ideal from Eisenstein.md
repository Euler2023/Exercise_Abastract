# Exercise R124: A Maximal Ideal from Eisenstein's Criterion

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 4.3
> Decide whether or not the polynomial
>
> $$
> x^4+6x^3+9x+3
> $$
>
> generates a maximal ideal in $\mathbb Q[x]$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> In a polynomial ring over a field, $(f)$ is maximal exactly when $f$ is irreducible.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> Apply Eisenstein's criterion with the prime $3$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Write
>
> $$
> f=x^4+6x^3+0x^2+9x+3.
> $$
>
> The prime $3$ divides every nonleading coefficient, $3$ does not divide the leading coefficient $1$, and $3^2=9$ does not divide the constant coefficient $3$. Eisenstein's criterion therefore shows that $f$ is irreducible in $\mathbb Q[x]$.
>
> Since $\mathbb Q[x]$ is a PID, a nonzero prime ideal generated by an irreducible polynomial is maximal. Equivalently, $\mathbb Q[x]/(f)$ is a field. Hence
>
> $$
> \boxed{(x^4+6x^3+9x+3)\ \text{is maximal}.}
> $$
