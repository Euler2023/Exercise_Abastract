# Exercise R51: A Triangular Polynomial Automorphism

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 3.6
> Let $R$ be a ring and $f(y)\in R[y]$. Prove that the map $R[x,y]\to R[x,y]$ defined by $x\mapsto x+f(y)$ and $y\mapsto y$ is an automorphism.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> Write down a map that subtracts $f(y)$ from $x$.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> Let $\Phi$ be the stated homomorphism and define $\Psi$ by
>
> $$
> \Psi(x)=x-f(y),\qquad \Psi(y)=y,
> $$
>
> with both maps fixing $R$. Then
>
> $$
> (\Psi\circ\Phi)(x)=x,
> \quad
> (\Phi\circ\Psi)(x)=x,
> $$
>
> and both compositions also fix $y$ and every coefficient. Since $R[x,y]$ is generated by $R,x,y$, both compositions are the identity. Thus $\Phi$ is an automorphism with inverse $\Psi$.
