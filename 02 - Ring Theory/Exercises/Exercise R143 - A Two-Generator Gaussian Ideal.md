# Exercise R143: A Two-Generator Gaussian Ideal

## Problem Statement

> [!question] Exercise 5.3
> Find a generator for the ideal of $\mathbb Z[i]$ generated by $3+4i$ and $4+7i$.

## Hints

> [!hint]- Hint 1
> In a Euclidean domain, the ideal generated by two elements is generated by their gcd.

> [!hint]- Hint 2
> Notice that both elements are divisible by $2+i$, and then find a Bézout combination.

## Solution

> [!success]- Solution
> We have
>
> $$
> 3+4i=(2+i)^2,\qquad
> 4+7i=(2+i)(3+2i),
> $$
>
> so $(3+4i,4+7i)\subseteq(2+i)$.
>
> Conversely, a direct Bézout combination is
>
> $$
> 2+i=(-1-2i)(3+4i)+(1+i)(4+7i).
> $$
>
> Hence $2+i$ belongs to the two-generator ideal, and therefore
>
> $$
> \boxed{(3+4i,4+7i)=(2+i)}.
> $$
>
> Any associate of $2+i$ is an equally valid generator.
